About

A sign shop run
by someone who
grew up in one.

Master Signs & Prints LLC is Ricardo “Ricky” Mendez's shop, working out of Davie and serving businesses across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach.

The trade came first

Ricky started in signs in high school, working alongside his father, who had built a South Florida sign business more than twenty-five years earlier. That is where the useful part was learned: how a file behaves at size, which material survives this sun, what a wall will actually hold, and how much of a job is decided before anyone cuts anything.

After his father passed away in 2025, Ricky started fresh under his own name. Master Signs & Prints LLC has been operating since the end of that year. The company is young. The decade of trade behind it is not, and we would rather state both plainly than round one of them up.

What we are trying to be

A sign partner, not a one-time sale. The work we want is the work that comes back: the next location, the next van, the next season's campaign. That only happens if the first job is handled properly and the phone gets answered afterwards.

Which is why we do not sell on being flawless. Sign projects involve materials, weather, municipalities, landlords and other trades — things go sideways occasionally in every shop that has ever existed. What we will promise is that you can reach someone, that the person you reach can make a decision, and that the response will be to sort it out rather than to explain whose fault it was.

Not this

The cheapest quote

We are competitive, and we are frequently not the highest number on the table. But cutting material spec to win a price is how a storefront gets replaced twice in three years.

Not this either

A faceless print portal

Upload, pay, receive whatever the file was going to produce. If your artwork has a problem, that model prints the problem. We call you.

This

Someone accountable

An owner who sees the jobs, answers the phone, and would rather keep a customer for the next five projects than win one on price.

Where we work

South Florida,
on the ground.

Based in Davie, working across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach — Miami, Doral, Hialeah, Aventura, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Plantation, Sunrise, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach and West Palm Beach among them. Coverage on a specific site is confirmed when we see the address, because access, permitting and install conditions are not the same everywhere.
