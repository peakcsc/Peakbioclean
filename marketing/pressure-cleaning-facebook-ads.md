# Pressure Cleaning Facebook Ads: South Florida Research + Scripts

Built on David's framework: every ad starts with research. Who, where, what problem, what pain, what people are saying.

## 1. Who we're targeting

**Primary: HOA homeowners, 35 to 65.** South Florida is HOA country. These buyers aren't shopping for "clean." They're trying to make a violation letter go away. Notices typically give 14 to 30 days to cure before fines.

**Secondary: Sellers and landlords.** Listing photos, move-out turnovers, tenant complaints. Speed matters more than price.

**Tertiary: Seniors and snowbirds.** Can't or shouldn't climb a roof or drag a pressure washer around. Snowbirds return in November through January to a house that grew black streaks all summer.

## 2. Where we're targeting

Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach. Start with dense HOA zip codes: Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Weston, Coral Springs, Davie, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Wellington, Doral, Homestead. Radius 15 to 25 miles around wherever the crew actually runs. Spanish-language versions for Miami-Dade and Hialeah.

## 3. What problem we're solving

Black streaks on tile and shingle roofs (algae, mostly Gloeocapsa magma, not dirt). Green and black mildew on driveways, pavers, pool decks and walls. Orange rust stains from well water irrigation, which pressure alone won't remove. Slippery pool decks and walkways.

## 4. Current pain points

The violation letter with a deadline and a fine. Embarrassment: "the worst house on the street." Fear of slipping, especially for older homeowners. A cheap guy already blasted it and the streaks came back in months. A cheap guy damaged paint or left zebra stripes on the driveway, then blamed the homeowner. No-shows and no callbacks.

## 5. What people are saying (negative talk)

Complaints cluster around three things: damage (stripped paint, etched concrete, green streaks on walls after a wash), it came back fast (high pressure removes the stain, not the root), and unprofessional operators (no show, no insurance, no accountability). Our ads should quietly answer all three: insured, soft wash where it matters, guaranteed.

## 6. The unfair advantage

We're a biohazard and crime scene cleanup company. No other pressure washer in South Florida can say that. "If we can clean a crime scene, your driveway doesn't stand a chance" is a hook that stops the scroll and proves standards in one line.

## Testing plan

Launch 4 to start: #1, #2, #3, #5. $15 to $20/day each, 5 to 7 days, Lead Form objective. Kill anything over your target cost per lead after $50 spend. Winner gets the budget; rotate in the rest.

Hook is everything in 5 to 10 seconds. The first 1.5 seconds must show either the dirt or the letter. No logo opens.

## Before launch, fix these

No pressure cleaning page exists on peakbioclean.com yet. Ads need a destination or a lead form. The site phone is a 407 (Orlando) number; South Florida buyers trust 305, 954 or 561. Get real before/after footage from local jobs; AI-looking footage kills trust on exactly the claim we're selling (it's actually clean).
