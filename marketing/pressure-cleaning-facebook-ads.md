# Pressure Cleaning Facebook Ads: South Florida Research + Scripts

Side business, owner operated. No roofs. Services: driveways, pavers, sidewalks, pool decks, patios, house walls, fences.

Built on David's framework: every ad starts with research. Who, where, what problem, what pain, what people are saying.

## 1. Who we're targeting

**Primary: HOA homeowners, 35 to 65.** South Florida is HOA country. A dirty driveway or stained wall gets a violation letter with 14 to 30 days to cure before fines. These buyers aren't shopping for "clean." They want the letter gone.

**Secondary: Sellers, landlords, Airbnb hosts.** Listing photos, move-out turnovers, guest reviews. Speed beats price.

**Tertiary: Seniors and snowbirds.** Can't or won't drag a pressure washer around. Snowbirds return November through January to a driveway that went black all summer.

## 2. Where we're targeting

Pick a 10 to 15 mile radius around home base. A side job lives or dies on drive time. Best HOA-dense zips in Broward and Palm Beach: Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Weston, Coral Springs, Davie, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Wellington. Miami-Dade: Doral, Kendall, Homestead. Spanish version for Miami-Dade.

## 3. What problem we're solving

Black and green mildew on driveways, pavers, sidewalks and pool decks. Dirty, streaked house walls. Orange rust stains from well water irrigation (pressure alone won't remove these; needs a rust remover). Slippery pool decks and walkways.

## 4. Current pain points

The violation letter with a deadline and a fine. "Worst house on the street" embarrassment. Fear of slipping, especially for older homeowners. A previous guy left zebra stripes on the driveway or stripped paint off the walls. Contractors who don't call back or don't show.

## 5. What people are saying (negative talk)

Complaints cluster around damage (stripped paint, striped or etched concrete), no-shows and ghosting, and companies that won't own a mistake. Typical driveway jobs in Florida run roughly $125 to $600, so price isn't the main objection. Reliability is.

## 6. The side job advantage

You're not a call center. You're the guy who answers the phone and shows up. That directly answers the #1 complaint. Put your face in the ads. "I answer my own phone" beats any logo.

## Testing plan

Start with 4 videos (#1, #2, #3, #6) and 4 statics (S1, S2, S3, S5). Budget can stay small for a side job: $10/day per ad set, 5 to 7 days. Use a Lead Form or "Send Message" objective; texting a photo for a quote is easier than any website. Kill anything that spends $40 without a lead. Shift budget to the winner.

First 1.5 seconds must show the dirt or the letter. No logo opens.

## Before launch

Get a local number (305, 954 or 561) on a free Google Voice line. Shoot real before/after on every job; your first three jobs are your ad library. Get general liability insurance before you say "insured." Post the same before/afters in local Facebook neighborhood and HOA groups for free leads while ads warm up.
