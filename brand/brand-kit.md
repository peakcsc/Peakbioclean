## Voice
Calm, competent, human. We show up for people on the worst day of their life — compassionate first, professional second. Never clinical, never sensational.

- Lead with reassurance and clarity before credentials.
- Plain language, short sentences.
- Always land: 24/7 availability, discretion, compassion, works with insurance.
- Name the real situation plainly and respectfully — don't euphemize into vagueness, don't sensationalize.
- First person plural ("we", "our team").
- Every post should answer "why trust us with this" without being salesy.
- Before/after content: relief and restoration, not shock value.

**Avoid:** gore/graphic imagery, dark humor, clickbait, corporate-speak, fear-based scare tactics, more than 2-3 emojis per caption.

## Caption formula
`Hook (real situation, 1 line) → what we did / what changed (2-3 lines) → reassurance + CTA (24/7 line, phone or link)`
