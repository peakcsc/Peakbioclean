# Google Search Console Setup — Peak Bio-Clean (DO THIS FIRST)
Why: your 241 pages can't rank until Google indexes them. GSC is how you tell
Google to crawl them. Free. ~15 minutes. The single biggest SEO action left that
you (not me) have to do.

## Step 1 — Add your site
1. Go to: search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with the SAME Google account you'll use for your Google Business
   Profile (or the one that manages it, once it's verified).
3. Click "Add property" → choose the "URL prefix" box (right side).
4. Enter exactly: https://www.peakbioclean.com
5. Click Continue.

## Step 2 — Verify ownership (easiest method)
Google will offer several verification options. Easiest for you:
- If it offers "Google Analytics" or "Google Business Profile" association, use
  it if available.
- Otherwise choose the "HTML tag" method:
  1. It gives you a `<meta>` tag that looks like:
     `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXXXXXX" />`
  2. Copy that whole tag and send it to me — I'll paste it into the site's
     `<head>`, push it, and then you click "Verify" in Search Console.

## Step 3 — Submit your sitemap (the important part)
Once verified:
1. In the left menu, click "Sitemaps."
2. In "Add a new sitemap," type: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click Submit.
This tells Google about all 241 pages at once.

## Step 4 — Request indexing of key pages (optional, speeds it up)
1. Top search bar → paste a page URL (e.g.
   https://www.peakbioclean.com/crime-scene-cleanup/)
2. Click "Request indexing." Repeat for your top 5–10 pages (homepage, your core
   services, /blog/, /service-areas/).

## Step 5 — Turn on AI eligibility
In Search Console, look for the "Search generative AI" setting (under Settings
or page experience). Make sure it's set to INCLUDE — this keeps you eligible to
appear in Google's AI Overviews / AI Mode.

- Google crawls your 241 pages → they become eligible to rank.
- Real data on the searches people actually use to find you (impressions,
  clicks, average position).
- After a few weeks, real data to measure whether this whole project worked.
