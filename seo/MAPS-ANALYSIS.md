## Why I'm confident reviews + GBP are the bottleneck, without live data
This isn't a guess specific to your industry — it's how Google's local ranking
algorithm is publicly documented to work everywhere: relevance, distance, and
**prominence** (review count/quality + overall web presence) are the three
official ranking factors. On-site schema affects relevance; it does nothing for
prominence. Native Screens' live test independently confirmed this exact pattern
in a directly comparable local-service market. There's no reason to expect
biohazard cleanup works differently.

## NAP consistency (claim/verify these once GBP is live)
Make Name + Address + Phone identical everywhere:
[ ] Google Business Profile
[ ] Bing Places (bingplaces.com)
[ ] Apple Business Connect (businessconnect.apple.com)
[ ] Facebook / Instagram (you already have these — verify phone matches)
[ ] BBB, Yelp, Nextdoor Business, local chamber of commerce

Inconsistent NAP confuses Google and hurts local rank. If you operate as a
service-area business with no public street address, declare "service area
business" and hide the address identically on every listing — never show it on
some and hide it on others.
