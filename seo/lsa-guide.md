## IF a matching or close-enough category exists — here's the process

### BEFORE YOU START — have these ready
[ ] Business name: Peak Bio-Clean
[ ] Business phone: (407) 758-0682 (a number you actually ANSWER — missed LSA
    calls hurt your ranking and waste money)
[ ] Business address / service area (statewide Florida, or your real bounded
    area if LSA requires one)
[ ] Florida business license number + proof
[ ] Proof of general liability INSURANCE (certificate PDF)
[ ] EIN / business details
[ ] A credit card for billing
[ ] Owner's info for a background check (Google uses a partner for this)
[ ] The Google account that manages your Google Business Profile

### STEP 1 — Start the application
1. On a computer, go to: ads.google.com/local-services-ads
2. Click "Get started" / "Sign up."
3. Sign in with the Google account that manages your GBP.

### STEP 2 — Confirm business basics and CHECK THE CATEGORY
1. Enter your business name: Peak Bio-Clean.
2. Search the job-category list for "biohazard," "crime scene," "trauma
   cleanup," and "disaster restoration." Note exactly what Google offers — this
   is the fact I couldn't verify for you.
3. If nothing fits, stop here and don't proceed with LSA — it isn't a channel
   for you right now.

### STEP 3 — Set your SERVICE AREA
Start tight (your actual fastest-response area), not statewide on day one — a
smaller area means cheaper, closer, higher-value leads while you learn what
converts. Expand once you know your close rate.

### STEP 4 — Business hours & lead types
Set hours to match reality — if you're genuinely 24/7, say so. Make sure calls
forward to someone who answers; speed protects both your ranking and the lead.

### STEP 5 — Upload license, insurance & pass screening
Upload your business license and liability insurance certificate, complete the
business + owner background check (Google's partner runs it, free and
standard), and submit for review. Approval can take a few days to ~2 weeks.

### STEP 6 — Set your budget
Set a weekly budget cap you're comfortable with. Google shows an estimated
lead range for that budget — it's an estimate, not a promise. You can raise,
lower, or pause any time.

### STEP 7 — After it's live: protect your money
- Answer every call fast — missed/slow responses lower ranking and waste spend.
- Dispute bad leads (spam, wrong number, out of area) in the LSA dashboard —
  Google refunds them if disputed within the allowed window.
- Reviews boost LSA rank too — keep the review drive going.
- Check the LSA app daily for new leads.

### PITFALLS TO AVOID
- Don't go statewide on day one.
- Don't use a phone you won't answer.
- Don't forget to dispute junk leads.
- Don't confuse LSA with regular Google Search Ads — LSA is the "Google
  Guaranteed" pay-per-lead ads at the very top.
