# Peak Bio-Clean — Review Drive Kit

**Goal:** go from 0 → 15+ Google reviews once your GBP is verified. This is the
single highest-ROI thing you can do for both your local map ranking and getting
named in AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI) — confirmed directly by a live test on
a comparable local-service business (Native Screens): businesses named in AI
answers had 44–240 reviews; a business with only 7 wasn't named once.

**An important difference from a typical home-services review kit:** your
customers are often grieving, in shock, or dealing with a property crisis — not
someone who just got a new pool enclosure. The timing and tone of a review ask
matters more here. Use softer timing and always make it feel fully optional.
Commercial/referral-partner contacts (property managers, funeral homes, adjusters)
are a different case — you can ask them more like a normal B2B relationship.

## ✅ Habits that actually move the number

- **Ask with care, not urgency.** For residential/family jobs, a few days'
  gap and gentle, optional wording matters more here than in most industries.
- **Commercial contacts can be asked faster** — property managers, funeral
  homes, and insurance adjusters are a normal B2B relationship.
- **Make it one tap.** A direct review link converts far better than asking
  someone to search for you.
- **Reply to every review** — brief and warm, good or bad.
- **Never pay for reviews or only ask people you know were happy, in writing**
  — against Google's rules and can get reviews removed. Ask everyone, let the
  quality speak.
- **A steady trickle beats a burst.** 2–4 reviews a month looks natural to
  Google; a sudden spike can look manufactured.
